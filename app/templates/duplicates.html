How Netrik decides

  • Merged automatically: the same hardware MAC address; a device without a MAC and a device with one that held the same IP address; the same specific hostname when no stable MAC contradicts it.
  • Never merged automatically: generic names (such as Galaxy-S23 or raspberrypi), random-MAC devices that were active at the same time, and two devices with different hardware MACs. One PC can have Wi-Fi and Ethernet, or these may be two machines, so a person decides.
